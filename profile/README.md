# Kavana Labs

**Kavana Labs** is an early-stage science and technology organization with a
**dual-arm structure**:

- An **impact-driven (non-profit) arm** focused on redefining STEM education and
  building scientific capacity in Africa.
- A **for-profit arm** focused on building sustainable technology products and
  delivering high-quality engineering solutions.

The for-profit arm exists to **financially sustain and scale the mission** of
the impact arm over the long term.

🌍 **Website:** https://kanvalabs.studio  
📍 **Base:** Ghana  
🧪 **Status:** Early-stage / Pre-revenue  

---

## 🎯 Vision

To help bring Africa to the **global stage of scientific and technological
civilization** by promoting **deep understanding, practical skills, and
experimentation**, rather than rote learning or shallow credentialism.

---

## 🌱 Impact Arm (Non-Profit)

The impact arm of Kavana Labs focuses on **STEM education and scientific
ecosystem development**, with an emphasis on:

- Practical, experiment-driven learning
- First-principles understanding of science and technology
- Community-based problem solving
- Youth development and long-term capacity building

### Key activities include:
- STEM bootcamps and hands-on workshops
- Conferences and technical talks (large and lightning formats)
- Blogs, newsletters, and educational publications
- Tutorials and learning content (YouTube, blogs, social platforms)
- Scholarships and sponsorship programs
- Competitions and hackathons with learning-focused prizes
- Long-term plans for labs and innovation hubs

### Priority audience:
While open to all, priority is placed on **youth**, with the long-term goal of
integrating programs into formal education systems:
- Primary school
- Lower and upper secondary school
- Tertiary institutions
- Early childhood education (long-term)

---

## 🏗️ For-Profit Arm

The for-profit arm of Kavana Labs focuses on **building sustainable technology
products and services**, which fund and enable the impact work.

### Core areas:
- **In-house products**
  - Scientific and technical platforms (e.g. *[Praxis](https://github.com/Kavana-Labs/praxis)*)
  - Education technology (EdTech) tools and infrastructure
- **Technology solutions for businesses**
  - Software engineering and systems development
  - Data-driven and research-informed solutions
  - Technical consulting and implementation

Products and services are developed with a strong emphasis on **engineering
rigor, clarity, and long-term value**.
